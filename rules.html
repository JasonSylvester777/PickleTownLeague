PickleTown Tournament Rules

Standard pickleball rules apply unless a PickleTown tournament rule below specifically sets the competition format.

Standard Pickleball Rules — Quick Guide

1. Serve

Serve underhand and diagonally into the opposite service court. The serve must clear the non-volley zone.

2. Two-Bounce Rule

The receiving team must let the serve bounce, and the serving team must let the return bounce. After those two bounces, either side may volley or play off a bounce.

3. The Kitchen

You may not volley while touching the non-volley zone or its line. Your momentum after a volley may not carry you into the kitchen.

4. Standard Scoring

Pickleball normally uses side-out scoring: only the serving side scores. A standard game is to 11 points and must be won by 2.

5. Line Calls

A ball touching a line is in, except a served ball touching the kitchen line, which is a fault.

6. Common Faults

Examples include hitting out, into the net, volleying before both required bounces, kitchen violations, a double bounce, or touching the net while the ball is live.

This is a practical tournament summary. For the complete official rules, visit USA Pickleball Rules.

Spring League Tournament Format

  1. The tournament is played as doubles: two registered players per team.
  2. The Spring League regular season runs for 9 weeks, beginning Monday, 28 September 2026.
  3. Only teams with payment status Paid are included in automatic fixture scheduling. Pending teams remain registered but are not scheduled until payment is confirmed.
  4. Each team is scheduled for a minimum of 2 and a maximum of 3 regular-season fixtures per week. Two fixtures is the normal weekly target; a third may be used where required for catch-up, balancing or late-entry fairness.
  5. Regular-season fixtures are scheduled Monday to Saturday between 18:00 and 20:00, subject to court availability.
  6. There are 4 courts available and fixtures are allocated to one-hour slots.
  7. The scheduler avoids the same opponent more than once in the same week and tries to avoid rematches against an opponent played during the previous 2 weeks. If there are too few teams, the least-recent repeat may be used.
  8. Confirmed fixtures for the following week are issued by email every Friday. Once that week's schedule has been emailed, it is treated as confirmed.
  9. Teams may still join the current Spring League through the end of Week 2, Saturday 10 October 2026. A newly Paid team is automatically allocated from the next available unlocked week.
  10. Registrations received after the Week 2 cutoff are not added to the current standings. They are automatically reserved for the Summer League and both players are notified by email.
  11. A team/player may only be registered once per season. Duplicate team names, emails or player telephone numbers are blocked by the registration system.
  12. During the one-hour fixture, teams play as many completed games as possible. Each game is played to 11, win by 2.
  13. At the end of the fixture hour, the team with the most completed games won wins the fixture.
  14. League points: Win = 3 points, Draw = 1 point each, Loss = 0 points.
  15. The live standings use league points first, followed by wins, game difference and games won as tie-break indicators.
  16. The top four teams after the regular season qualify for the finals programme.
  17. Semifinals and the Grand Final take place on Sunday, 29 November 2026.
  18. The appointed umpire controls the official score record. The result becomes official once the umpire finalises the fixture in the tournament system.
  19. Players should arrive at least 10 minutes before their scheduled start. Umpires should arrive at least 15 minutes before their assigned match.

Communication & Scheduling

Confirmed fixtures for the following week are emailed every Friday to both registered players. Each weekly email includes the opponent, date, time, court and assigned umpire (or To Be Confirmed if an umpire has not yet been allocated). Match-day communication is also sent at approximately 07:00 and includes the match details, fee and current league position. The organiser receives a daily fixture summary for WhatsApp reminders.

Rescheduling & Forfeits

  1. If a team cannot play its scheduled fixture, that team must submit a reschedule request through the Team Match Centre by 14:00 on the original match day.
  2. The opposing team receives an email with the proposed date and time and may either accept or propose a different permitted date/time.
  3. The teams may continue proposing alternatives until a mutually agreed slot is reached. All reschedules remain subject to court availability and the permitted Monday–Saturday evening slots.
  4. A mutually agreed reschedule must be confirmed by 16:00 on the original match day. If no agreement has been reached by 16:00, the fixture is recorded as a draw and each team receives 1 league point.
  5. A team may also declare in advance that it cannot play. This declared forfeit is recorded as a draw – 1 point each.
  6. A no-show is treated differently. If a team does not arrive without an agreed reschedule or prior declared forfeit, the absent team loses the fixture, the opposing team receives 3 league points, and the absent team is responsible for the R50 umpire fee.
  7. Reschedule/forfeit outcomes do not add artificial games won or lost to the game-difference tie-break.
  8. If a registered umpire is also a tournament player, the system avoids assigning that person to umpire during a time slot in which their own team is playing.
Match-day email: Players and umpires receive their reminder at approximately 07:00. The organiser also receives the prepared WhatsApp reminder list for that day's fixtures.

Fair Play

Players and umpires are expected to act respectfully, make honest line calls, accept the umpire's administrative score record and resolve questions calmly. The organiser may clarify an operational rule where a situation was not anticipated, provided the same interpretation is applied consistently to affected teams.
