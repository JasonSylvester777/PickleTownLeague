PickleTown Tournament Rules

Standard pickleball rules apply unless a PickleTown tournament rule below specifically sets the competition format.

Standard Pickleball Rules — Quick Guide

1. Serve

Serve underhand and diagonally into the opposite service court. The serve must clear the non-volley zone.

2. Two-Bounce Rule

The receiving team must let the serve bounce, and the serving team must let the return bounce. After those two bounces, either side may volley or play off a bounce.

3. The Kitchen

You may not volley while touching the non-volley zone or its line. Your momentum after a volley may not carry you into the kitchen.

4. Standard Scoring

Side-out scoring applies: only the serving side scores. A standard game is to 11 points and must be won by 2.

5. Line Calls

A ball touching a line is in, except a served ball touching the kitchen line, which is a fault.

6. Common Faults

Examples include hitting out, into the net, volleying before both required bounces, kitchen violations, a double bounce, or touching the net while the ball is live.

This is a practical tournament summary. For the complete official rules, visit USA Pickleball Rules.

Spring League Tournament Format

  1. The tournament is played as doubles: two registered players per team.
  2. The Spring League regular season runs for 9 weeks, beginning Monday, 28 September 2026.
  3. Regular-season fixtures are scheduled Monday to Saturday between 18:00 and 20:00, subject to court availability.
  4. There are 4 courts available and fixtures are allocated to one-hour slots.
  5. Teams are paired through the scheduling system, with repeat opponents avoided where reasonably possible.
  6. During the one-hour fixture, teams play as many completed games as possible. Each game is played to 11, win by 2.
  7. At the end of the fixture hour, the team with the most completed games won wins the fixture.
  8. League points: Win = 3 points, Draw = 1 point each, Loss = 0 points.
  9. The live standings use league points first, followed by wins, game difference and games won as tie-break indicators.
  10. The top four teams after the regular season qualify for the finals programme.
  11. Semifinals and the Grand Final take place on Sunday, 29 November 2026.
  12. The appointed umpire controls the official score record. The result becomes official once the umpire finalises the fixture in the tournament system.
  13. Players should arrive at least 10 minutes before their scheduled start. Umpires should arrive at least 15 minutes before their assigned match.

Communication & Scheduling

Registered teams receive their fixture schedule by email once the schedule is published. Match-day communication is sent at approximately 07:00 and includes the opponent, date, time, court, assigned umpire, match fee, current league position and the 14:00 reschedule cutoff. The organiser also receives a daily fixture summary for WhatsApp reminders.

Rescheduling & Forfeits

  1. If a team cannot play its scheduled fixture, it must use the Team Match Centre to propose an alternative permitted date and time.
  2. The opposing team must accept the proposed change by 14:00 on the original match day.
  3. Reschedules remain subject to an available court and the tournament's permitted Monday–Saturday evening slots.
  4. If the request is still unresolved at 14:00, the requesting team forfeits and the opposing team receives 3 league points.
  5. A team may also voluntarily record a forfeit through the Team Match Centre.
  6. A forfeit counts as a win/loss for league points but does not add artificial games won or lost to the game-difference tie-break.
  7. If a registered umpire is also a tournament player, the system will avoid assigning that person to umpire during a time slot in which their own team is playing.

Fair Play

Players and umpires are expected to act respectfully, make honest line calls, accept the official score record and resolve questions calmly. The organiser may clarify an operational rule where a situation was not anticipated, provided the same interpretation is applied consistently to affected teams.
