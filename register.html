Fee summary
Once-off registration: R100 per player / R200 per team.
Each scheduled match: R50 per player / R100 per team.
Bat hire: R20 per bat per hour.

Payment status after registration: Pending.
PickleTown will update the team to Paid once payment is confirmed, or Cancelled if the registration is withdrawn.

Scheduling: Only Paid teams are included in fixtures. Confirmed fixtures for the following week are emailed each Friday. Teams may join the current Spring League through the end of Week 2; registrations received after that are automatically reserved for the next Summer League.

One registration per team/player: The system blocks duplicate team registrations and prevents a player from being registered more than once in the same season.
